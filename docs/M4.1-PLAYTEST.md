# M4.1 mobile playtest pass

Baseline: M4 `ae553185928d2f8149385222fd4209dae472fc96`; same feature branch / PR #4. No production merge or promotion. No production-art migration.

## Player control

Defensive Shockwave is available from the start of every run, independent of hero ownership or equipped spells. Its tuning is centralized in `dist/shockwave.js`:

- Cooldown: 18 simulation seconds, beginning at activation.
- Charge: 0.18 seconds; travel: 0.65 seconds.
- Front: existing lane Y=535 toward Y=285 (250 units).
- Knockback: normal 80, runner 92, armored 32, boss 6.4 units.
- Zero damage, no stun, no new rewards or RNG calls. Each living enemy is affected once per wave. X is unchanged; Y is bounded at zero. The current straight-lane movement remains authoritative; future curved paths require a path adapter before using this mechanic there.
- Cooldown and travel freeze with pause, inspection, upgrade selection and hidden-tab simulation pause. Speed controls advance the same equal simulation steps. Active-run state is not persisted, matching existing save behavior.

The reserved renderer shows the actual authoritative front, including charge and falloff, at every quality. Two planes and one instanced debris draw use fixed resources. Debris caps are 3/7/12 on Low/Standard/High and zero under load or reduced motion. Debris consumes the existing decorative budget; essential front visibility does not. Canvas fallback draws the same front. The HUD exposes ready, pressed, active and cooldown states with a radial fill and Hebrew label.

## Special hierarchy and hero identity

Existing leader powers and collection spells retain all damage, targeting, timing, charge costs and effects. The ten leaders still use their existing four gameplay archetypes; this does not add new hero abilities or change Briar's inherited control into invented healing. Explicit presentation attribution accompanies projectiles, damage, burn and slow sources. Station hits clear stale attribution. Only real healing callouts use healing feedback.

Player powers gain a reserved release-sigil pool, larger muzzle/projectile/contact forms, wider connected arcs, directional fragments, bounded camera response, stronger gunner recoil and special damage styling. Brass/sol/umbra retain mechanical force with separate audio rhythms; Ember/cinder use fire/forge signatures; Volt and Prisma use jagged versus refractive actual-target connections; Nova/Aurora share cold mechanics with different release scale/signatures; Briar uses green leaf/control feedback and nature attack/impact/death audio. No visual chain invents a target. Existing automatic cold control remains lower audio priority than a manual power. No global hit-stop, new dynamic lights, distortion buffers or post-processing.

Specials with immediate authoritative damage remain immediate: their release cue overlaps the hit, rather than delaying simulation for an animation. Shockwave alone has an actual charge/travel phase.

## Audio mix

Music phrase and direction are unchanged. Wind gain falls .025→.003, forest .008→.001, rain maximum .035→.006 and fire maximum .018→.003; noise cutoffs are narrowed (wind 160 Hz, forest 900 Hz, rain 950 Hz, fire 320 Hz). This substantially reduces sustained hiss without adding assets or voices.

Special signatures combine transient, tonal body and short filtered tails; Shockwave has pressure, air and debris layers. Ordinary voices remain capped at 22 and all transient sources at 28. Player powers have priority 2 and can replace lower-priority voices, including priority-1 cues; ordinary/impact cues cannot evict player powers. Master/music/SFX controls and lifecycle remain the M4 architecture. Final speaker mix approval still needs real-device listening.

Browser fixture: `scripts/verify-m41-browser.cjs`, extending M4's mobile/day/night/rain/quality/stress/settlement/restart checks with native Shockwave activation, radial cooldown, repeat activation, paused cooldown, 4-class contact, muted buses and a captured active front.
